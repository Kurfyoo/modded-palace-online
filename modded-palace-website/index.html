Goal

Get rid of all of your cards before everyone else. Cards are played from your hand first, then your face-up cards, then your face-down cards. Players who run out finish in order; the last player left is last place.

Setup

Every player is dealt 3 face-down cards and 6 cards in hand, then keeps 3 of the 6 face-up on the table. The rest of the deck is split into two draw piles. One card from each draw pile starts the two discard piles (a Joker is reshuffled back; a 10 destroys its pile right away).

You choose the number of players (human or bot), the number of 52-card decks and the number of Jokers before the game starts.

Taking a Turn

There are two discard piles. On your turn you choose one of them and play at least one card on it. Any extra cards you play in the same turn must go on that same pile. Press End turn when you are done.

Your hand is topped up to 3 cards after each play while the draw piles last. Face-up cards can only be played once your hand and both draw piles are empty, and face-down cards after that (you play them blind).

The first card you play must be equal to or higher than the top card of the pile (Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13, Ace 14). An empty pile accepts anything.

After your first card, you can keep stacking on the pile you just played on, as long as each card is the same value as the top card or exactly one higher.

Special Cards

2: Can be played on anything as your first card, and later only on top of an Ace or another 2.

10: As the first card of your turn it can be played on anything. Any 10 destroys the whole pile it is played on, moving it to the destroyed pile.

Joker: Wild. You choose its value from 2 to 14 when you play it. A Joker in hand before you play can also be spent to evolve an ability. Face-Down Jokers count as Aces.

Ace: The highest card. Only an Ace, a 2, a 10 or a Joker can go on top of an Ace.

Picking Up

If you cannot play, or your first card is illegal, you pick up both discard piles and your turn ends. An illegal card after your first play is blocked and ends your turn without picking anything up. A face-down card that turns out to be illegal also makes you pick up both piles.

Characters, Abilities, and Evolutions

Each player has a character with abilities. Some abilities can evolve: spend a Joker from your hand before you play any card and your turn ends, but the ability becomes its evolved version. Evolved abilities keep the base ability's behavior unless stated otherwise.

Abilities whose name contains (Domain) are domains and follow Domain Clashing. See the character descriptions.

Multi-use Rules

Abilities are built from these reusable rules. Characters can gain them from their abilities, and God of Jujutsu lets its owner use any of them.

Top Card Discard

On your turn, remove the top card from one chosen discard pile and move it to the destroyed pile. This rule can be used once per round.

Stack Destroy

When you play a card with the same value as the top card you played on that pile, destroy the entire pile, including both cards. This rule can trigger multiple times in a round.

I-T

This player is immune to all effects from other players.

Quick Grab

When another player plays a specific card value, this player can add that card and add it to their own hand.

Soul Swap

Two players swap all their cards, including face-up and face-down cards.

Card Swapping

This player can swap a specific card for another as long as they can see the card.

Reveal

Every player this is applied to has to reveal one card of their choice that is hidden from other players. This lasts until the card is played.

Rescue Draw

Once every 2 rounds you can choose to draw 2 cards from the draw pile or if the draw pile is gone you can take 1 card of your choice from the destroyed pile.

Full Hand

This player picks up all of their face-up and face-down cards and adds them to their hand.

Domain Clashing

Any ability with (Domain) in its name is a domain. If anyone opens a domain, anyone with a domain, even an upgraded one they haven't unlocked, can instantly open theirs to clash. Without the domain unlocked, you can decrease its refinement by 1, expend a Joker from your hand, or exchange one of your upgraded abilities.

  • Every round that passes while clashing, the refinement of all clashing domains decreases by 1; at 0 the domain breaks.
  • A player who has their turn canceled or picks up the piles has their domain shatter instantly.
  • A domain with greater refinement than an opponent's shatters that opponent's domain at the end of the round.
  • Each turn while clashing you may draw a card: a royal (Jack, Queen, King) blocks the next refinement decrease, a Joker adds 1 refinement, and a 2-5 removes 1 refinement.
  • Domain effects are disabled while domains are clashing.
  • With 3 or more domains open, a player who isn't clashing can expend a Joker to decrease one domain's refinement by 1.
  • A domain stays open until it breaks, shatters, a player picks up the piles or has a turn canceled, or its time limit runs out (usually 3 rounds, set per domain).
  • An "open" domain can't break during a clash, but if an opponent's refinement is higher, their domain's effect still lands.

Cleave

This player can use top card discard once per round.

Related rule: Top Card Discard

Spider Net Cleave

Your top card discard from Cleave gains 5 uses per round and now whenever it causes a pile to lose all of its card the other pile will be destroy for free and you can replace both piles for free forcing them to place 1 card from their hand in each pile.

World Cutting Slash

Whenever this player plays a King, they can choose a card to delete from the game, then choose another player and increase their Cut Meter by 1. At 9, that player is eliminated and gets last place.

Waffle Cutting Slash

Whenever this player uses World Cutting Slash, it counts as using it 3 times. This player's Kings can be played on top of any card regardless of when in the turn it is.

Enchain

Once per game, this player can click this ability to force themselves into soul swapping with another of this player's choice. Then, this player will be forced to play 2 cards in a row, while playing those 2 cards this player will be immune to all other players' effects.

Related rule: Soul Swap

Enchain

Allows the player to soul swap with other players with no restrictions besides those immune to soul swap. During your 2 cards played from soul swapping you can completely ignore card values.

Related rule: Soul Swap

Binding Vow

In exchange for passing up a turn, any of your cards can be played as a king.

Binding Vow Merchant

Any of this player's cards can be stacked on top of a king.

God of Jujutsu

Once per round, this player can use 1 multi use rule of their choice. This player is also completely immune to having other players swap cards with them or use soul swap on them.

Gojo

Hollow Purple

This player gains the ability to stack destroy.

Related rule: Stack Destroy

Max Purple

When this player plays 3 cards in a row it will activate stack destroy as if they stacked 2 cards in a row.

Limitless Barrier

Whenever this player plays an Ace, they will gain a passive for however many they stacked. For 1 Ace, the player will gain I-T for 2 rounds. For 2 Aces, they will gain all of the previously listed abilities along with being able to pick up one of their face-down cards. For 3 Aces, the player will be able to pick up all of their face-down cards, along with I-T being applied for 3 rounds. Should 4 Aces be played, this player will be able to replace their hand with cards from the deleted pile along with this ability instantly being upgraded.

Related rule: I-T

Perfected Limitless

When this ability is evolved this player will gain I-T for the rest of the game along with gaining the Quick Grab ability for Aces.

Related rules: I-T, Quick Grab

Lapse Blue

Related rules: Card Swapping, Reveal

Reversal Red

The user can use card swapping with the card values of 6 and 9 now along with gaining rescue draw.

Related rules: Card Swapping, Rescue Draw

Six Eyes

Once per turn, this player can choose to either view one of their face-down cards or see someone else's hand.

Mastered Six Eyes

This player gains full hand along with being able to check any other player's cards at the start of their turn. Before drawing they are allowed to view the top 3 cards in both draw piles and choose 1 of them to draw.

Related rule: Full Hand

Simple Domain

Once per game, This player grants themselves a domain refinement of 3. This player can also 1 time per round discard a card in their hand to remove a negative effect from themselves.
